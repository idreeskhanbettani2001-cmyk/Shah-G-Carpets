Rules for choosing the perfect rug for your room

Whether living on your own or with a family, your living room is an important space. Selecting the perfect rug at Shah G Carpet is a crucial decision. Here are some rules to consider:

  • Ensure that the rug is the right size for the space.
  • The rug's color and pattern should complement the existing decor.
  • Match the style of the rug to the room, traditional or modern.
  • The shape of the room and the rug should complement each other.
  • Lighting and the room's surroundings can affect how the rug looks.
  • Choose a rug that you love and that reflects your personality.
  • Consider the material of the rug.
